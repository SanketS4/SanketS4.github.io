Learn & Spot privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-03

Learn & Spot is an independent spot-the-difference learning game for school learners in Standards 1 to 10, published by the Learn & Spot developer (“we”). This policy explains what the Android and iOS apps and the browser version do with information. Contact: sanketsuryawanshi904@gmail.com.

In short
  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • The player’s name and Standard stay on the device and are never sent to us or to advertisers.
  • We do not run a server that collects game data.
  • Ads are child-directed and non-personalized, provided by Google.
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Information stored on the device

No account is required. The first name or nickname and school Standard entered on the welcome screen are stored only on your device, are used only to greet the player and choose puzzles, and are never sent to us or to advertisers. Found differences, hints, lives, completed levels, completion counters, ad timestamps and reward receipts are stored locally on your device or browser. We do not operate a server that receives this puzzle progress. There is no cloud backup.

Advertisements

The Android and iOS app includes Google Mobile Ads. A single adaptive banner may appear on results screens. Google test IDs are the default in all builds; live ads require explicit publisher configuration. After Families settings are verified, an interstitial may appear when leaving every second completed level, with at least 90 seconds between fullscreen ads by default. When lives run out, an optional rewarded ad can add exactly one life after the SDK confirms the reward. Closing without a reward adds nothing. Free restart and Home remain available. Internal test builds show only Google test ads, including test interstitial and rewarded ads; in public releases fullscreen ads stay disabled until Families settings are verified. No App Open ads or gameplay banners are requested. The browser version does not show this native ad SDK. Ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment, under-age consent, non-personalized ads and G-rated content. Consent information is checked before SDK initialization; ads remain off when consent information is unavailable or requires an unsupported adult flow. The game can be played offline; ads require a network connection.

Non-personalized ads do not mean that no information is processed. Google documents that its SDK can collect and share IP address, product interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers, depending on configuration and SDK behavior. Advertising identifiers are blocked in our Android release configuration. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy for provider details, uses and retention.

Children’s privacy

Learn & Spot is made for children. We do not ask children for an email address, phone number, photo, address or any other contact details, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children on our own systems. The only name the app asks for is a first name or nickname, which never leaves the device. Parents and guardians who have questions, or who believe a child has sent us personal information by email, can contact sanketsuryawanshi904@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Deleting information

Because game data is kept only on the device, you control it directly: open Grown-ups → Reset progress to clear saved levels, or uninstall the app (or clear browser storage) to remove all game data, including the player’s name. The last fullscreen-ad timestamp is retained after a reset to avoid showing repeated ads. We hold no copy of this data, so there is nothing for us to delete from a server.

Permissions and learning content

The game does not need your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location to play. Images are bundled in the app. Learning themes draw on NCERT and CBSE resources; illustrations and puzzle text are original. The app is not endorsed by NCERT or CBSE.

Support and external links

If you email support, the information you choose to send is handled through email services to respond to your request. Do not include a child’s personal details in support messages. Requests to open reference websites or an email app pass through an adult-facing step. External services have their own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

This policy may change when the app or its services change; the “last updated” date above will be revised. For privacy or support questions, email sanketsuryawanshi904@gmail.com.
